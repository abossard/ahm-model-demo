# Scene 5 — Closing

**1:55–2:00.** One shot. The brief says nothing is needed from the demo here, and that's correct.
This is a title card. The only thing to get right is the loop.

## Shot 1 — Title card and loop

*Health Models icon → "Health that powers intelligent operations." → Microsoft logo → fade out. The
video must loop: the end state transitions back toward faint telemetry beneath the surface, tying
into Scene 1's data ocean.*

1. Open on <img src="./icons/03528-icon-service-Monitor-Health-Models.svg" width="20" alt=""> [`Monitor-Health-Models`](./icons/03528-icon-service-Monitor-Health-Models.svg), matched in size and position to the final frame of Scene 4, so the two scenes read as one continuous move.
2. Title: "Health that powers intelligent operations."
3. Microsoft logo — the corporate brand asset, not from the Azure icon set.
4. **The loop.** Don't fade to black. Fade the icon down while faint telemetry particles rise, at the
   colour grade and camera height established in Scene 1 shot 3. The last frame here and the first
   frame of Scene 1 have to be interchangeable.
5. Check the loop by playing the cut twice back to back before sign-off. A loop that only works once
   isn't a loop.

*"architectural diagrams, training materials, or documentation"*.

If this ships as marketing rather than training or documentation, that use is not covered and needs
explicit written permission from Microsoft. Settle it before distribution. See
